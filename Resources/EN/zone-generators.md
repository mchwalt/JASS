The sound sources — everything that makes tone.

- Holds the oscillators (OSC 1–3), SUB, NOISE, STRING-KARPLUS, WAVETABLE and CROSS MOD.
- These feed the rest of the rack; without at least one enabled generator there is no sound.

**Group header controls**
- **Enable** — bypasses/enables every visible module in this group at once.
- **↺ Reset** — restores this group's *default module selection* (which modules are shown, their order and zone). It does not change any knob values.
- **ⓘ Info** — this help.
