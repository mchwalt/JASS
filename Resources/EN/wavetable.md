Wavetable oscillator that scans through a table of waveforms.

- **BANK** — selects the table; **LOAD WAV** imports your own.
- **POS** — morphs through the table — sweep it for evolving timbres.
- **FREQ** — pitch.
- **AMP** — level.
- **VOICES** — adds detuned unison copies.
- **DETUNE** — spreads them for width.
