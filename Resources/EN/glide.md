Portamento — slides the pitch smoothly from the previous note into the new one. Works polyphonically (with multiple notes at once): chord notes are matched by pitch order so each voice (each individual note) glides to its nearest target.

- **TIME** — glide duration: 0 = off (instant), higher = a slower, more pronounced slide.
- **BEND** — range of the pitch strip / wheel in semitones, up and down alike: 0–24, default 2; 0 = the strip does nothing. Each layer has its own. Show GLIDE (MODULES menu) to reach it; it works with GLIDE off, although the module then looks dimmed.
