Sweeping comb-filter effect — the module works as either a phaser or a flanger.

- **TYPE** — *Phaser* (notches swept by allpass stages, airy/vocal) or *Flanger* (short modulated delay, jet-like whoosh).
- **RATE** — sweep speed.
- **DEPTH** — how far the sweep travels.
- **FB** (signal fed back) — feedback: intensity/resonance of the effect (higher = more pronounced, metallic).
- **MIX** (wet/dry blend) — how much effect is blended against the dry signal.
