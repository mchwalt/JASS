Two complete sounds at once — layer **A** and layer **B**, played from one keyboard.

- **A / B** (keys **1** / **2**) — which layer you edit; in **Single** also which one plays. B's modules have a violet header. Both lit: the rack shows A and every change goes to B too — knobs move B by the same amount, so B keeps its offset.
- **MODE** — **Single**: the lit layer plays (switch sounds with 1 / 2; a layer you leave rings out). **Dual**: every key plays both. **Split**: keys below SPLIT play B, from SPLIT up A — the keyboard shows both ranges.
- **SPLIT** — the split key, one semitone per move. Or click **SPLIT** on the keyboard (key **3**) and play a key; click again to cancel. Outside Split it switches MODE to Split.
- **A LVL / B LVL** — each layer's level.
- **A PAN / B PAN** — each layer's balance: centre leaves the layer as it is, a side turns the other side down.
- **Loading a preset** (LOAD, F-keys) follows A / B: **A** loads the preset and keeps your layer B; **B** puts only the preset's sound into B; **both** load the whole file, both layers — a preset without a layer B (all older ones) keeps your B.

ARP and STEP SEQ play layer A (in Single with B: layer B). RANDOM rolls the layer you edit (both when both are lit); RESET in the header returns to Init: A a bright saw stack, B a dark square bass — a Split plays two different sounds right away.
