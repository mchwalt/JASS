Two complete sounds at once — layer **A** and layer **B**, played from one keyboard.

- **A / B** (keys **1** / **2**) — which layer you edit and hear; a layer you leave rings out. Press **B** once: A and B; press **B** twice quickly (double-click): B only — the same with **A** on the way back. B's modules have a violet header; each layer shows the modules it uses. Both lit: the rack shows A and every change goes to B too — knobs move B by the same amount, so B keeps its offset.
- **SPLIT on the keyboard** (key **3**) — split on / off: keys below the split key play B, from it up A, whatever A / B shows. **Double-click** (3, 3 quickly), then play a key: it becomes the split key; click again to cancel.
- **SPLIT** — the split key, one semitone per move.
- **A LVL / B LVL** — each layer's level.
- **A PAN / B PAN** — each layer's balance: centre leaves the layer as it is, a side turns the other side down.
- **Loading a preset** (LOAD, F-keys) follows A / B: **A** loads the preset and keeps your layer B; **B** puts only the preset's sound into B; **both** load the whole file, both layers — a preset without a layer B (all older ones) keeps your B.

ARP and STEP SEQ play the layer set by their **TARGET** (Auto: the selected layer, both with A+B, B in a Split — there the left hand feeds the pattern and the right hand plays A live). RANDOM rolls the layer you edit (both when both are lit); RESET in the header returns to Init: A a bright saw stack, B a punchy saw bass — a Split plays two different sounds right away.
