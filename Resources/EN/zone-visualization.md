The meters — see the sound, they never change it.

- Holds the OSCILLOSCOPE (waveform over time) and the SPECTRUM (frequency content).
- Purely passive displays; disabling them only freezes/blanks the view and costs no CPU.

**Group header controls**
- **Enable** — freezes/enables every visible display in this group at once.
- **↺ Reset** — restores this group's *default module selection* (which displays are shown, their order and zone). It does not change any knob values.
- **ⓘ Info** — this help.
