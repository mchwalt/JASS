The meters — see the sound, they never change it.

- Holds the OSCILLOSCOPE (waveform over time) and the SPECTRUM (frequency content).
- Purely passive displays; disabling them only freezes/blanks the view and costs no CPU.

**Group header controls**
- **Enable** — freezes/enables every visible display in this group at once.
- **Reset** (circular arrow) — puts this group back to your **default layout** (`RackLayout.json` in the JASS folder — **Save as default** in the MODULES panel writes it). It does not change any knob values.
- **Module ↺** (circular arrow in a module's title bar) — the module back to the loaded preset; **Shift + ↺** — factory values (the icon turns amber with a dot while Shift is held). **Double-click** a knob — that knob back to the loaded preset.
- **Info** (circled i) — this help.
