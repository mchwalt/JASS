Live view of the **final output waveform** over time — exactly what leaves the synth, after every
master stage (compressor, stereo/binaural modes, ROOM, master volume). **Stereo:** left and right
are shown as **two plots side by side** (**L** blue, **R** orange); when the signal is effectively
mono they would be identical, so a single full-width plot is shown. The time-base can be zoomed to
inspect the wave shape.
