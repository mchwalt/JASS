Shapes brightness.

- **TYPE** — Lowpass (removes highs → darker) or Highpass (removes lows → thinner).
- **CUTOFF** (filter brightness) — corner frequency where filtering begins; shows the LFO ring when the LFO targets it.
- **RESO** (emphasis at cutoff) — resonance: emphasises the frequencies right at the cutoff — high values ring or whistle.
