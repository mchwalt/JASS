Plucked-string physical model (Karplus-Strong).

- **PLUCK** — (or the spacebar) excites the string.
- **FREQ** — the string's pitch.
- **AMP** — level.
- **DAMP** — how fast the tone dies away (low = long sustain, high = short/muted).
- **STR** — stretch: detunes the overtones for a more metallic or looser character.
