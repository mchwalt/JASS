Adds harmonic grit and saturation.

- **TYPE** — flavour: Soft Clip (warm, gentle), Hard Clip (harsh, aggressive), Foldback (buzzy, complex).
- **DRIVE** (intensity) — how hard it's pushed (more = dirtier, louder harmonics).
- **MIX** (wet/dry blend) — blends the distorted signal against the clean one.
