West-coast wavefolding (extra harmonics) — folds the waveform back on itself for bright, metallic harmonics.

- **DRIVE** (fold intensity) — how much folding (more = more added harmonics).
- **SYM** (fold offset) — symmetry: offsets the fold for asymmetric, hollower tones.
- **MIX** (wet/dry blend) — blends against the dry signal.
