One-shot pitch sweep fired at every note-on — for kicks, zaps, lasers and risers.

- **AMOUNT** — sweep depth in semitones. Positive sweeps **down** to the base pitch (kick/drum, zap); negative sweeps **up** (laser, riser). 0 = off.
- **TIME** — how long the sweep takes to settle to the base pitch (decay).

Affects the oscillators, wavetable and sub — not the plucked string.
