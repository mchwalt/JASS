Core oscillator (defaults to the played pitch).

- **WAVE** — timbre: sine = pure, sawtooth = bright/buzzy, square = hollow, triangle = soft.
- **FREQ** — pitch.
- **AMP** — level in the mix.
- **VOICES** — stacks detuned unison copies for a fatter sound.
- **DETUNE** — spreads the voices apart (wider, chorus-like).
- **FB** — self-FM feedback; higher morphs sine → sawtooth → gritty inharmonic tones.
