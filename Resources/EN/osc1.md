Core oscillator (defaults to the played pitch).

- **WAVE** (the basic tone shape) — timbre: sine = pure, sawtooth = bright/buzzy, square = hollow, triangle = soft.
- **FREQ** (how high or low) — pitch.
- **VOICES** (stacked detuned copies, fatter) — stacks detuned unison copies for a fatter sound.
- **DETUNE** (slight pitch offset) — spreads the voices apart (wider, chorus-like).
- **FB** (self-FM: oscillator fed into itself) — self-FM feedback; higher morphs sine → sawtooth → gritty inharmonic tones.
- **AMP** (loudness) — level in the mix.
