Shapes each note's volume over time. Disable for a flat, organ-like level.

- **ATK** — attack: fade-in speed (0 = instant/percussive, high = slow swell).
- **DEC** — decay: fall from the peak down to the sustain level.
- **SUS** — sustain: the held level while a key is down.
- **REL** — release: fade-out time after the key is let go.

**CURVE (title bar)** folds the envelope display away — the module shrinks to its knob row and the rack packs the modules below upwards. Click again and the curve returns, shifting them back down.
