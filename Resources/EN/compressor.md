Master-bus compressor — evens out level and glues the whole mix together. Runs once on the summed output (not per voice).

- **THRESH** — level (dB) above which compression kicks in. Lower = more of the signal is compressed.
- **RATIO** — how hard it clamps above the threshold (2:1 gentle … 20:1 near-limiting).
- **ATK** — how fast it reacts to peaks (short = snappy control, long = lets transients through).
- **REL** — how fast it recovers after the signal drops.
- **GAIN** — make-up gain to bring the compressed level back up.
