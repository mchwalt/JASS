Vocal formant filter (vowel colour, "ah/oh/ee") — shapes the tone like a human voice by emphasising vowel resonances.

- **VOWEL** — morphs the vowel shape across A → E → I → O → U.
- **RESO** (peak emphasis) — resonance: how sharply the formants are emphasised (higher = more vocal, pronounced).
- **MIX** (wet/dry blend) — how much filtered signal is blended against the dry signal.
