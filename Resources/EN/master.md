The synth's final output stage.

- **VOL** — the final output volume.
- **TEMPO** — the internal BPM that drives Tempo-Sync (the note-division rates in LFO and DELAY). Inside a DAW the host tempo overrides this value.

**Computer keyboard**

Play notes with the middle/top letter rows (`a w s e d f t g y h u j k`). Two extra keys shift the playable range:

- **Z** — one octave *down*
- **X** — one octave *up*
- **Space** — re-pluck the Karplus string

The on-screen keyboard must have focus (it grabs focus on launch; click it once if the keys stop responding).
