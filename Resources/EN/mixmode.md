Couples two oscillators for metallic / FM (frequency modulation) timbres (tone colors). Disable the module to just add the oscillators normally.

- **MODE** — coupling type: *RingMod* multiplies the two signals (bell-like, clangorous sidebands — extra overtone frequencies); *FM* makes one modulate the other's pitch (harmonically rich, can turn aggressive).
- **SRC A** / **SRC B** — which two oscillators are coupled (always kept different).
